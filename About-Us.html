About US

The origins of Yumora

Yumora was founded in 2015 by Yuru, Moksha, Aryssa and Arfa. The brand name was formed by combining all our names together.

We first started as a student run business on campus at NYP. Orders were taken from our instagram page at the time. Eventually, word got around outside of the school about our business. Selling our snacks to students and teachers was tough but we persevered doing what we love. Due to the sudden rise in popularity, we expanded our business to sell outside of NYP. Fast forward to today, we now have 7 outlets across Singapore. So that everyone in Singapore will have the chance to consume healthy and affordable snacks.

Our mission & vision

Our journey began after noticing how many people were eating unhealthy snacks simply because healthy snacks were too expensive, a pattern that could lead to poor health down the line. We discovered that a granola bar costs 6 dollars in stores like 7-eleven! We strongly believe that snacks should not compromise our well-being. So instead of cutting out the snacks, we decided to create healthy high-quality snacks made with utmost care.

From yoghurt to smoothies, we have got you covered on a healthy snack that won't break your bank. Our goal is simple to give everyone access to snacks that are both healthy and affordable.
